Terms

Short and plain. By joining Valor you agree to these.

Who can use it

You must be 18 or older. Groups are invite-only.

Valor is not money

No real money, ever. Valor cannot be bought, sold, cashed out or sent to anyone, and there are no prizes. Any IOU you add to an oath (like "loser buys dinner") is between friends; the app never touches it.

Be a good sport

  • Be kind. No bullying, harassment, threats or hate.
  • No nudity or sexual content, and nothing illegal.
  • Only post photos you took, and only of people who are happy to be in them.
  • Report anything that breaks these rules; it is hidden while the operator looks.

The operator may remove content and accounts that break these rules, and may ban someone from Valor.

A free test

Valor is a free test with no guarantees. It may change, lose data, or stop, and the operator may end it at any time. Use it for fun and for keeping your word, not for anything you cannot afford to lose.

Questions

Write to hello@vnlabs.dev. How your data is handled is in the privacy note.
